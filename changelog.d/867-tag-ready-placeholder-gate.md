- `tools/self/tag-ready.sh [REV]` is the pre-tag check for a left-over placeholder marker, the bracket plus the word PLACEHOLDER (dir #867; spelled out because the tool greps this file's own section): it reads the committed `docs/release-history.md` and `CHANGELOG.md` at REV (default HEAD), exits 1 and prints `path:line:text` while one stands, and refuses (exit 2) on a bad argument, an unresolvable rev or a missing file. Release-audit Phase 7 and the publishing checklist's tag step run it on the SHA about to be tagged. It is deliberately not a suite leg: a cut PR may carry the marker until the audit's GO record fills it.
